Shader snippets

Open Markdown

A snippet is a small piece of GLSL that your addon adds at a named point of the shaders of Viewpoint. Use it to change a surface, a vertex or a colour, for example to make surfaces wet or to tint the scene.

Permission: shaders.

Add a snippet

Section titled “Add a snippet”

A snippet is the body of a function. Viewpoint declares the function for the point, with the point’s parameters. Your snippet reads and changes the parameters.

  • add takes the point, a name for messages, the body, and the programs that it runs in. Give no program to run it in every program that has the point.
  • Load a long snippet from your data assets, then add it. See Assets.
  • Close the registration to take the snippet out. When your addon goes off, Viewpoint takes out all its snippets.
  • Read the values of your addon by their names, for example wetness above. See Shader values.

The points

Section titled “The points”
PointParametersPrograms
surfaceinout Surface s (vec3 albedo, vec3 normal, vec4 light, bool foliage), vec3 local, vec3 pos, vec3 eyegbuffer, forward, far shell, far shell band
vertex.worldinout MeshVertex v (v.pos in scene space)gbuffer, forward, puddle
vertex.endinout vec4 clipPositionevery program with a vertex stage, but those with tessellation or a geometry stage
gbuffer.endinout GBuffer g (vec4 albedo, vec4 normal, vec4 light, vec4 velocity)gbuffer, far shell band, model, vehicle, grass, snow layer, snow layer tessellated, snow wake
colour.endinout vec4 colourlight, far shell, far

surface and vertex.world are in the middle of a shader. A Shaderpack that replaces their file keeps their marker. If a Shaderpack does not keep it, your snippets at that point do not run, and a message on the Addons page says so.

At gbuffer.end, a program without a velocity output ignores what you write to g.velocity.

Rules

Section titled “Rules”

Viewpoint checks each snippet when you add it. If the snippet breaks a rule, add throws an IllegalArgumentException that gives each reason.

  • A snippet is at most 16 KiB. The snippets of one addon are at most 64 KiB together.
  • The braces, brackets and parentheses of a snippet close in the snippet.
  • A snippet has no # lines.
  • A snippet has no while or do loops. A for loop counts with numbers towards a number: for (int i = 0; i < 8; i++), or for (int i = 8; i > 0; i -= 2).
  • A snippet writes no images or buffers, and calls no atomic or barrier functions. It declares no uniforms or blocks.
  • A snippet uses none of the names of Viewpoint’s shaders: no name of u, v or a followed by a capital letter, and no name that starts with vp_ or addon_. Of the names that start with gl_, it can read only gl_FragCoord, gl_FrontFacing, gl_PointCoord, gl_VertexID and gl_InstanceID.

The graphics driver compiles the snippet. If it fails, your snippets at that point stop, a message on the Addons page says so, and console.txt has the driver’s error. The other addons and Viewpoint go on.

When a change applies

Section titled “When a change applies”

When an addon with snippets goes on or off, Viewpoint compiles the programs with those points again.

  • In a single-player game, the game pauses until the programs are ready. A dark layer covers the screen, under the Esc menu, and the bar at the bottom left shows “Compiling addon shaders” and the progress.
  • In a multiplayer game, the game does not pause. Only the bar shows.

Note

After the programs are ready, a snippet costs only its own GPU time in each frame. Your card’s Performance tab shows it.
