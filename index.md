For an AI agent: the list of every page is [llms.txt](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/llms.txt).

- [Start](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/start/introduction.md): What a package is. How to make the smallest addon. The samples.
- [For players](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/players/managing-addons.md): Install, order and switch packages. Read a label.
- [Concepts](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/concepts/package-kinds.md): The three kinds of package, the descriptor, the states, the versions and the order.
- [API 1.0](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/api/view-state.md): The view state, settings, services, the camera, the fight, models and shader values.
- [Patching](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/patching/managed-patching.md): Declare hooks into game methods and into Viewpoint. Viewpoint installs, orders and lists them.
- [Labels & consent](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/trust/labels.md): Permissions, labels, consent and updates.
- [Performance](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/performance/assets-and-budgets.md): The memory pools, the estimates, and how to measure your package.
- [Resource packs](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/resource-packs/format.md): Models, tiles, sounds and data, with no code.
- [Shaderpacks](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/shaderpacks/format.md): Change how Viewpoint draws the world, with GLSL files and no Java.
- [Reference](https://project-viewpoint.github.io/viewpoint-docs/reference/descriptor-keys.md): Descriptor keys, labels, permissions, the internals list and a glossary.
